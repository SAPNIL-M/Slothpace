## 📷 The 3 Must-Have Screenshots (+ 1 Bonus)

Use **`Windows Key + Shift + S`** (Windows Snipping Tool) to take high-resolution screenshots of `http://localhost:5173/`.

### 1. `01-morning-brain-dump.png`
* **What to capture:** The initial screen.
* **How to stage it:** Click one of the 1-click presets (e.g. *“💻 Dev Morning Chaos”*) so the text area is filled with realistic chaotic tasks, showing the starting time picker and the green **“Pace My Day With Open-Source AI 🦥”** button.
* **Caption in DEV post:** *“Morning Brain Dump: Dumping raw, chaotic thoughts without stressing about structure.”*

---

### 2. `02-paced-schedule-dashboard.png`
* **What to capture:** The generated Schedule Dashboard after clicking Pace.
* **How to stage it:** 
  - Ensure the **🌿 Branch Progress (Sloth Marker 🦥)** is visible at the top.
  - Expand 1 or 2 task cards to show the decomposed micro-steps, the `+10m Sloth Buffer` button, and the **⚡ Deep Focus** / **🌿 Easy Sprint** pills.
  - Check off 1 micro-step so the progress bar shows movement.
* **Caption in DEV post:** *“The Paced Schedule: Local AI classifies cognitive load, decomposes micro-steps, and schedules gentle deadlines.”*

---

### 3. `03-gentle-focus-timer.png`
* **What to capture:** The **Focus Mode** modal.
* **How to stage it:** Click **Focus Mode** on any deep task (e.g., *“Fix JWT token refresh”*). Make sure the large countdown timer, active micro-step, `+5m Buffer`, and `60s Breathe` buttons are clearly shown.
* **Caption in DEV post:** *“Gentle Deadline Focus Companion: Structured pacing without panic, featuring panic-free buffers and audio ambience.”*

---

### 4. `04-export-markdown.png` *(Optional Bonus)*
* **What to capture:** The Export modal showing the generated Markdown ready for Obsidian / Notion.
* **Caption in DEV post:** *“One-click export to Markdown for personal knowledge bases and daily standup notes.”*

## 🎥 Video Demo Guide (Strongly Recommended!)

A short video or animated GIF dramatically increases your chances of winning badges because judges can see the app in action within 30 seconds.

### Quick Recording Specs:
* **Length:** 30 to 45 seconds (keep it snappy!)
* **Free Tool Options:**
  * **Windows Game Bar** (`Win + G` or `Win + Alt + R` to instantly record the browser tab).
  * **[Loom.com](https://www.loom.com/)** (super easy, 1-click sharing link).
  * **ScreenToGif** (if you want a crisp animated `.gif` to drop right into the post).

### 🎬 Recommended 35-Second Video Walkthrough Script:
1. **[00:00 - 00:08]**: Start at `http://localhost:5173/`. Click **“💻 Dev Morning Chaos”** to auto-fill the tasks.
2. **[00:08 - 00:15]**: Click **“Pace My Day With Open-Source AI 🦥”**. Watch the local AI parse the tasks and reveal the paced schedule and sloth tracker.
3. **[00:15 - 00:23]**: Check off a micro-step (hear the chime sound and watch the sloth move along the branch).
4. **[00:23 - 00:32]**: Click **“Focus Mode”** to open the gentle countdown timer. Click **“+5m Buffer”** to show how easy it is to handle delays without panic.
5. **[00:32 - 00:35]**: Close modal and click **“Export Schedule”** to show the clean Markdown.

### Where to paste the Video Link:
* If you use **Loom** or **YouTube (Unlisted)**, simply paste the link into the `DEV_POST_SUBMISSION.md` under the Demo section!
* DEV automatically embeds YouTube and Loom videos when you paste the URL.
